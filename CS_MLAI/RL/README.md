[NTU-DL-22sp](https://speech.ee.ntu.edu.tw/~hylee/ml/2022-spring.php): Great course, short and friendly introduction. It helps you grasp the core ideas of RL, but remains somewhat shallow and isn’t ideal for deep understanding.

[Westlake-MathFoundationRL](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1sd4y167NS): Great course, friendly for beginners, with clear logic and solid depth.

[UCLA-CS260R-26wi](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1KdCCszXqyBQWa7r3Rm0EN6Ci5PLSo0s3Ul75siO8k_k): Very high-quality homework. Very confusing lectures. The lectures had unclear logic and may not be suitable for beginners.
