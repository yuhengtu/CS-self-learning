SEU-Calculus1&2-21: Great course with wide coverage. The learning experience depends heavily on the lecturer. The exams build solid calculus skills, you’ll remember $(x^2)' = 2x$ in your whole life. It’s for sure not as good and intuitive as MIT 18.01 or 18.02, but the course quality is higher than 99% of the courses at SEU.
